What most 10 speed owners believe“Lifter problems are an old truck thing. Mine’s new, so I’ll deal with it later.”

New truck, same locking pin. Owners plug it in early, not after the tick

DFM is GM’s newer version of AFM, and it’s busier, not quieter. GM’s own technician publication, TechLink, describes it deciding about 80 times a second, across 17 different cylinder patterns. To shut a cylinder off, special lifters release a small spring loaded locking pin, then lock it again. Every switch is another cycle on that pin, from the first mile.

Changing your oil early keeps the oil fresh. It doesn’t give the pin a day off, because switching is how the system is designed to run. Owners are already posting about lifter repairs on 2022 and 2023 trucks, and quotes commonly land between $4,000 and $8,000.

Here’s the honest part. FullForce 10 Speed is prevention, not a repair. It stops the switching so those pins stop cycling, which lowers the risk. It can’t promise a lifter will never fail, and it won’t undo wear that’s already happened. If you’re already hearing a tick or feeling a misfire, get it looked at first. The trade on the other side: expect about 1 mpg less, roughly what DFM was saving.
